# Wellperion Adult Lesson Inquiry Form (English)

**Form Title:** Wellperion Private Sports Club — Adult Lesson Inquiry
**Form Description:** Interested in private or group lessons at Wellperion? Fill in the details below and our program consultants will reach out to arrange a session tailored to your schedule and goals.

> **Language Integration Note:** Add a hidden field `Language` with fixed value `EN` so all responses merge into the existing Korean response sheet under a unified language-distinguishing column.

---

## Fields

### 1. Full Name
- **Field type:** Short answer
- **Required:** Yes
- **Label:** Full Name
- **Placeholder:** e.g., Kim Namwook

---

### 2. Mobile Phone Number
- **Field type:** Short answer
- **Required:** Yes
- **Label:** Mobile Phone Number
- **Placeholder:** e.g., 010-0000-0000

---

### 3. Age Group
- **Field type:** Multiple choice (single selection)
- **Required:** Yes
- **Label:** Age Group
- **Options:**
  - 20s
  - 30s
  - 40s
  - 50s
  - 60 and above

---

### 4. Program of Interest *(select all that apply)*
- **Field type:** Checkbox (multiple selection)
- **Required:** Yes
- **Label:** Program of Interest
- **Options:**
  - Personal Training (PT)
  - Swimming Lessons
  - Aqua Exercise
  - Golf Lessons
  - Squash Lessons
  - Pilates

---

### 5. How Did You Hear About Us?
- **Field type:** Multiple choice (single selection)
- **Required:** Yes
- **Label:** How Did You Hear About Us?
- **Options:**
  - Member referral
  - Instagram / Social media
  - Online search
  - Building / Residential community notice
  - Other

---

### 6. Preferred Lesson Time
- **Field type:** Checkbox (multiple selection)
- **Required:** No
- **Label:** Preferred Lesson Time *(select all that apply)*
- **Options:**
  - Early morning (before 8 AM)
  - Morning (8 AM – 12 PM)
  - Afternoon (12 PM – 5 PM)
  - Evening (5 PM – 9 PM)

---

### 7. Instructor Preference
- **Field type:** Multiple choice (single selection)
- **Required:** No
- **Label:** Instructor Preference
- **Options:**
  - No preference
  - Prefer a specific instructor (please specify in the comments below)

---

### 8. Additional Requests or Comments
- **Field type:** Short answer (paragraph)
- **Required:** No
- **Label:** Additional Requests or Comments
- **Placeholder:** Please share any specific requirements, physical conditions, or preferences our instructors should be aware of.

---

### 9. Privacy Consent
- **Field type:** Checkbox (single item — must be checked to submit)
- **Required:** Yes
- **Label:** Consent to Collection and Use of Personal Information

**Consent Text:**

> In accordance with the **Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) of the Republic of Korea**, Wellperion Co., Ltd. ("the Company") collects and processes your personal information as follows.
>
> **Purpose of collection:** Processing lesson inquiries; scheduling consultations and trial lessons; program matching.
>
> **Items collected:** Name, mobile phone number, age group, program interest, preferred lesson time, instructor preference, additional requests.
>
> **Retention period:** Retained for 1 year from the date of collection, or until the purpose is fulfilled, whichever is earlier. Upon expiration, all personal data will be destroyed without delay.
>
> **Third-party disclosure:** Your personal information will not be shared with third parties without your separate consent, except as required by applicable law.
>
> You have the right to refuse consent; however, doing so may prevent us from processing your inquiry.
>
> ☐ **I have read and agree to the above terms for the collection and use of my personal information.**
