**Form Title:** Wellperion Private Sports Club — Youth (WSC) Lesson Inquiry
**Form Description:** Enroll your child in Wellperion's exclusive youth sports programs. Please provide the details below and our WSC program team will contact you to discuss the best fit.

### 9. Privacy Consent
- **Field type:** Checkbox (single item — must be checked to submit)
- **Required:** Yes
- **Label:** Consent to Collection and Use of Personal Information (including minor's data)

**Consent Text:**

> In accordance with the **Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) of the Republic of Korea**, Wellperion Co., Ltd. ("the Company") collects and processes personal information as follows. As this form concerns a minor, the legal guardian is required to provide consent on the child's behalf.
>
> **Purpose of collection:** Processing youth program inquiries; scheduling program consultations and trial sessions; program matching for the child.
>
> **Items collected:** Child's name, child's age, guardian's name, guardian's contact number, relationship to child, program interest, additional requests.
>
> **Retention period:** Retained for 1 year from the date of collection, or until the purpose is fulfilled, whichever is earlier. Upon expiration, all personal data will be destroyed without delay.
>
> **Third-party disclosure:** Personal information will not be shared with third parties without separate consent, except as required by applicable law.
>
> The guardian has the right to refuse consent; however, doing so may prevent us from processing the inquiry.
>
> ☐ **I am the legal guardian of the child named above, and I have read and agree to the above terms for the collection and use of personal information.**
