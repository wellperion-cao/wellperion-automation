# Wellperion Membership Inquiry Form (English)

**Form Title:** Wellperion Private Sports Club — Membership Inquiry
**Form Description:** Thank you for your interest in Wellperion, Seoul's premier private sports club. Please complete the following to schedule your exclusive club tour and consultation.

> **Language Integration Note:** Add a hidden field `Language` with fixed value `EN` so all responses merge into the existing Korean response sheet under a unified language-distinguishing column.

---

## Fields

### 1. Full Name
- **Field type:** Short answer
- **Required:** Yes
- **Label:** Full Name
- **Placeholder:** e.g., Kim Namwook

---

### 2. Mobile Phone Number
- **Field type:** Short answer
- **Required:** Yes
- **Label:** Mobile Phone Number
- **Placeholder:** e.g., 010-0000-0000
- **Note:** Used for consultation scheduling only.

---

### 3. Areas of Interest *(select all that apply)*
- **Field type:** Checkbox (multiple selection)
- **Required:** Yes
- **Label:** Areas of Interest
- **Options:**
  - Health & Wellness Management
  - Swimming
  - Golf
  - Sauna & Recovery
  - Private Lessons (PT / Aqua / Pilates / Squash)

---

### 4. How Did You Hear About Us?
- **Field type:** Multiple choice (single selection)
- **Required:** Yes
- **Label:** How Did You Hear About Us?
- **Options:**
  - Member referral
  - Instagram / Social media
  - Online search
  - Building / Residential community notice
  - Other

---

### 5. Preferred Tour Date
- **Field type:** Short answer (date)
- **Required:** No
- **Label:** Preferred Tour Date (and Time, if applicable)
- **Placeholder:** e.g., 2026-06-15, afternoon preferred
- **Note:** Our consultants will confirm availability within one business day.

---

### 6. Health & Wellness Goal
- **Field type:** Short answer (paragraph)
- **Required:** No
- **Label:** Your Health & Wellness Goal
- **Placeholder:** Briefly describe what you hope to achieve through membership (e.g., weight management, stress relief, sport performance).

---

### 7. Privacy Consent
- **Field type:** Checkbox (single item — must be checked to submit)
- **Required:** Yes
- **Label:** Consent to Collection and Use of Personal Information

**Consent Text:**

> In accordance with the **Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) of the Republic of Korea**, Wellperion Co., Ltd. ("the Company") collects and processes your personal information as follows.
>
> **Purpose of collection:** Scheduling and conducting membership tours and consultations; responding to membership inquiries.
>
> **Items collected:** Name, mobile phone number, areas of interest, preferred tour date, wellness goal.
>
> **Retention period:** Retained for 1 year from the date of collection, or until the purpose is fulfilled, whichever is earlier. Upon expiration, all personal data will be destroyed without delay.
>
> **Third-party disclosure:** Your personal information will not be shared with third parties without your separate consent, except as required by applicable law.
>
> You have the right to refuse consent; however, doing so may prevent us from processing your inquiry.
>
> ☐ **I have read and agree to the above terms for the collection and use of my personal information.**
