FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I visit Wellperion without a reservation?

Tours and consultations are by appointment only. Walk-in registration is not available. Inquiries: wellperion.com/ko/inquiry

How does membership work?

Membership is capacity-limited. When full, a waiting membership is offered; details are given during consultation.

What are the opening hours?

Weekdays 06:00-22:30, weekends and public holidays 08:00-20:00. Closed on New Year's Day, Seollal and Chuseok holidays, and the 2nd and 4th Sunday of each month.

Where is it?

413 Seobinggo-ro, Building 101, B1 (Hannam-dong), Yongsan-gu, Seoul. Phone +82-2-6261-1200.

Which facilities are available?

Swimming, training, golf (GDR and QED bays), squash, gymnastics, pilates, sauna and spa, all in one space. See the Spaces section above for details.

Are lessons also reservation-only?

Yes. All lessons, including P.T, swimming, gymnastics, golf, squash and G.X, run by advance reservation only; walk-in registration is not offered.

How large is the club?

A single space of about 3,000 pyeong (approx. 9,900 m²) in Hannam-dong, Yongsan-gu, Seoul.

Is Wellperion a membership sports club in Hannam-dong?

Yes. Wellperion is a members-only sports club in Hannam-dong, Yongsan-gu, Seoul, run within a capped membership of 1,000 members. When capacity is full, a waitlist membership is offered.
